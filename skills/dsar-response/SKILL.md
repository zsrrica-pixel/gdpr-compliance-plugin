# Data subject request handling

1. Identify which right is being invoked and verify the requester's identity proportionately.
2. Deadline: one month from receipt, extendable by two months for complex or numerous requests (tell the requester within the first month).
3. Check for valid grounds to refuse or limit (manifestly unfounded/excessive, third-party rights, legal retention duties, exemptions for erasure under Art. 17(3)).
4. List the systems and processors to search; give a collection checklist.
5. Draft the response letter: confirmation of processing, copy of data, and the Art. 15(1) information (purposes, recipients, retention, source, rights) for access requests. For refusals, state reasons and the right to complain.
6. Log the request, dates and outcome.

## Sequencing and exemptions
- **Combined access + erasure:** freeze deletion, compile and send the access copy first (or together), then erase.
- **Erasure exemptions (Art. 17(3)):** retain data required by law (e.g. invoices and payment records under tax/accounting rules, typically several years depending on member state: confirm the local period) or needed for legal claims. Restrict retained data, tell the requester what is kept, why and until when, and delete when the period ends.
- **Art. 19:** notify processors and recipients of the erasure.
- **Backups:** document that backups are overwritten on the normal cycle and the data is isolated until then.
- **Secure delivery:** expiring encrypted link or encrypted file, password sent separately.
- Output a collection checklist (system, data, action), the response letter with `[placeholders]` for unknown facts, a log table, and a list of missing facts to ask.
